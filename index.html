About the game

Marble Run does not require you to create an account or log in. The game does not directly ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, postal address, phone number, or any user-generated content. To keep the game free, it may use software development kits (SDKs) and advertising or analytics services required by our publisher, Voodoo, and may display advertisements.
